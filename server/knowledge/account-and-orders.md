# Orders and Your Account

## Order confirmation
After you place an order you receive a confirmation email with your order number, which looks like ORD-1001. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within 15 minutes.

## Checking order status
You can ask this chat for your order status. Give the order number and the email address used for the order. We never share order details without both.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it, or any time before it ships. After it ships you cannot cancel, but you can return it once it arrives. To cancel, contact support with your order number.

## Changing items in an order
We cannot add or swap items after an order is placed. Cancel the order and place a new one if it has not shipped.

## Your account
You can create an account with your email address to save your details for faster checkout. To reset your password use the Forgot password link on the login page. Guest checkout is also available.

## Deleting your data
To delete your account and personal data, contact support from the email address on the account. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.
