# Contact and Support Hours

## Chat assistant
This chat assistant is available 24 hours a day and can answer questions about shipping, returns, payments, sizing, products and your orders.

## Human support team
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern time. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day. We aim to reply within one business day.

## Email
You can email the support team at support@hannes-demo.example. Include your order number to get help faster.

## When to ask for a person
Ask for a person if you have a damaged item, a payment problem, a delivery that is more than 3 business days late, or an issue the assistant cannot solve. The assistant will open a ticket and pass the chat to the team.

## Business details
Hannes & Co. is an online clothing store. This help center is a sample created for a demo store.
