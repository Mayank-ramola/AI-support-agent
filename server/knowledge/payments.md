# Payments and Promo Codes

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery, checks or bank transfers.

## When you are charged
Your card is charged when you place the order. If an item goes out of stock after you order, we cancel that item and refund it automatically.

## Taxes
Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and is shown before you pay.

## Promo codes
You can use one promo code per order. Codes cannot be combined and do not apply to gift cards or shipping fees. Codes must be entered at checkout, because they cannot be added after the order is placed.

## Payment problems
If your payment is declined, check the card number, expiry date and billing address, then try again or use another method. If you were charged but did not receive an order confirmation email, contact support with the last four digits of your card and the time of purchase. Do not place the order twice.

## Security
We never store full card numbers. Payments are processed by our payment provider over an encrypted connection.
