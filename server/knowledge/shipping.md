# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options and prices
Hannes & Co. ships within the United States and Canada. We do not ship to other countries yet.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and costs $4.99. Orders over $75 get free standard shipping.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. Express is not available for orders shipped to P.O. boxes.

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern time on a business day are packed and handed to the carrier the same day or the next business day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship on the next business day. Processing time is not included in the delivery estimates above.

## Tracking your order
When your order ships you receive an email with a carrier name and tracking number. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear. You can also ask this chat for your order status: give your order number and the email used for the order.

## Delayed or lost packages
If your package has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support. We will open an investigation with the carrier and send a replacement or refund if the package is confirmed lost.

## Changing your address
You can change the delivery address only before the order has shipped. Contact support as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has shipped the address cannot be changed.
