# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have the original tags attached.

## How to start a return
Contact support with your order number and the items you want to return. We email a prepaid return label within one business day. Pack the items, attach the label and drop the parcel at any carrier location.

## Return shipping cost
Return shipping is free if the item is damaged, defective or we sent the wrong item. For other returns the label costs $6.99, which is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method. After we receive and inspect your return, the refund is issued within 2 business days. Your bank may take another 5 to 7 business days to show it.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or color if it is in stock. Start an exchange the same way as a return and say which size or color you want. If the new item is not in stock we refund you instead.

## Items that cannot be returned
For hygiene reasons socks cannot be returned once opened. Items marked Final Sale cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift cards are not refundable.

## Damaged or wrong items
If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem. We will send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
