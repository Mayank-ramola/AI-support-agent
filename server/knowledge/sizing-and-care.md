# Sizing and Garment Care

## Available sizes
Most clothing is available in S, M, L, XL and XXL. Socks come in one size that fits shoe sizes 6 to 12.

## Fit guide
T-shirts and slim fits run true to size. Hoodies and casual wear have a relaxed fit. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger size for hoodies and sweatpants, and the smaller size for slim fits if you want a closer fit.

## Size chart for tops (chest, in inches)
S fits 34 to 36, M fits 38 to 40, L fits 42 to 44, XL fits 46 to 48, XXL fits 50 to 52.

## Size chart for pants and sweatpants (waist, in inches)
S fits 28 to 30, M fits 32 to 34, L fits 36 to 38, XL fits 40 to 42, XXL fits 44 to 46.

## Washing and care
Machine wash cold with similar colors and tumble dry on low. Do not bleach. Wash hoodies and jackets inside out to protect the print and fabric. Iron on low heat if needed, and never iron directly on a print.

## Wrong size?
If the size does not fit you can exchange it within 30 days. See the returns policy for how to start an exchange.
